Beef Stew Recipe

Image from thechunkychef.com

Just when you want a good hearty meal

This is one of our go to foods in the winter, it's all you need to feel warm and cozy after a hard day's work. We use pressure cooker to cook it faster but it's not a must.

Note: Add 20-30 minutes to cooking time if you are not using a pressure cooker.

Ingredients

  • 2 onions, big slices
  • 4 small/medium potatoes
  • 4 large carrots, diced
  • 4 tomatoes, cut in half
  • 2 chopped celeries
  • 3-6 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tablespoons of olive oil
  • 1-1.5kg lean beef, cut to small pieces(up to you to decide how small)
  • 1-2 teaspoons of black pepper and red pepper.
  • 3 teaspoons of salt

Steps

  1. You're gonna add the beef, carrots and onions in first and cook them a bit without water until they are half cooked.
  2. Then add the rest of the ingredients and add water until it covers about less than half of the pot.
  3. Cook in medium heat for about 40 minutes. In time you will get a sense of smell for when it's done.
  4. Once it's done, open the lid and put a fork through a chunk of beef, if it's easily coming apart, it's ready.
