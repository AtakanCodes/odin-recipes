Crispy Air Fried Potatoes Recipe

Image from haylenrecipes.com

Let's get into the crunch of the business!

When I want to have a quick, no-hassle yet tasty meal, I get to slicing the 'taters(but we aren't going to boil em' mash em' stick em' in a stew today)

Note: Seasoning is essential unless you want to solely depend on the sauce of your choosing.

Ingredients

  • 3 big potatoes
  • Half a glass of sunflower oil
  • Salt, ground cayenne pepper and coriander

Steps

  1. After slicing the potatoes to your choosing of shape (I always prefer wedges), wash them thoroughly to get rid of the starch.
    (This will ensure potatoes won't stick to the basket.)
  2. Dry the potatoes and put them to the air fryer basket.
  3. Pour the oil on your 'taters and mix it in the basket so the oil is evenly distributed.
  4. Cook for 45 minutes at 195c.
  5. When the cooking is halfway done, turn the potatoes over.
  6. Once it's done cooking, transfer the potatoes into a mixing bowl.
  7. Add all the seasonings and mix.
  8. For sauce, pretty much anything goes. I prefer garlic mayo and ketchup but I also go with spiced up yogurt if I want to watch my calories.

I hope you enjoy it as much as I do. It's simple but it's all you need when you aren't looking for a full course meal.
